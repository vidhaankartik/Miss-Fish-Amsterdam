MISS FISH 2026 — UPDATE YOUR EXISTING NETLIFY SITE
===================================================

GOOD NEWS: No new site. Your current link and QR code stay the same:
https://luminous-eclair-a2730e.netlify.app/

1. Extract this ZIP. Open the `miss-fish-2026-upgrade` folder in VS Code.
2. In Netlify -> your existing site -> Environment variables, create:
   NETLIFY_API_TOKEN = [your own private Netlify personal access token]
   Keep it secret; do not put it in your files or share it.
3. From VS Code -> Terminal (inside the project folder), run:
   npx netlify-cli login
   npx netlify-cli link
   (IMPORTANT: choose the EXISTING luminous-eclair-a2730e project)
   npx netlify-cli deploy --prod --no-build --dir=public --functions=netlify/functions
4. Open your original URL and click View results to test.

A real public leaderboard requires the `results` Netlify Function.
Just dragging public/ onto Netlify will not reliably deploy this function.
